Jmap AIO privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Jmap AIO is a map app that shows live ships, trains, transit, traffic cameras, earthquakes, and world events from public data feeds. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

Summary

  • Jmap AIO has no accounts and no servers of its own.
  • The app is free and shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. Google AdMob collects device and usage information to show ads, as described in Ads.
  • An optional one-time purchase through Google Play removes the ads. Google Play handles the payment, and the developer receives only the order record from Google Play.
  • The app doesn't ask for permission to use your device's location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files.
  • The app connects directly from your phone to the public data sources listed below.

Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't included in device backups.

  • Keys you enter. Some optional layers need a free key that you get from that data provider. The app saves each key so you don't have to enter it again.
  • Settings. The last map position you viewed, which layers you turned off, and whether you've seen the welcome screen.

To delete a key, tap the 🔑 button, open that key's box, and tap Remove. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Information the data sources receive

To show live data, the app requests it directly from each source over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Like any website, each source receives your phone's IP address and the time of the request. The app also identifies itself as "alexc-jmapaio" to these sources, which some of them require. The app doesn't send your name, email, or device identifiers to these sources.

  • Map: OpenFreeMap supplies the base map, so it receives the map area you're viewing.
  • Keys: Each key goes only to the provider it belongs to, and only to request that provider's data.
  • Links: If you tap a link, such as a news article or a source's website, it opens in your phone's browser or email app. That site's own privacy policy applies there.

Ads

Until you buy Remove ads, the app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen. To choose and show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob collects and shares information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Your approximate location, based on your IP address
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as app crashes and ad loading performance

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent before any ads load. To change your choice, tap the ⓘ button, and then tap Ad privacy choices.
  • To reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, use your phone's settings. On most phones, go to Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads.
  • The Remove ads purchase stops all ads in the app.

Purchases

The Remove ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details. Google Play gives the developer an order record with the product, order number, date, price, and country. The developer uses it only to manage the purchase, such as for refunds, and to meet tax and accounting rules. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase each time it starts, so it can turn ads off. Google Play's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

The developer keeps no data about you except Google Play order records, which are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Google keeps ad data under its own retention rules. Data on your phone stays until you remove it or uninstall the app. To ask about your order records, email the address in Contact.

Children

Jmap AIO is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13. The app doesn't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
