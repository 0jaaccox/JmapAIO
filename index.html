Jmap AIO privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Jmap AIO is a map app that shows live ships, trains, transit, traffic cameras, earthquakes, and world events from public data feeds. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

Summary

  • Jmap AIO has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer doesn't collect, receive, or store any information about you.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • The app doesn't access your device's location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files.
  • The app connects directly from your phone to the public data sources listed below.

Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't included in device backups.

  • Keys you enter. Some optional layers need a free key that you get from that data provider. The app saves each key so you don't have to enter it again.
  • Settings. The last map position you viewed, which layers you turned off, and whether you've seen the welcome screen.

To delete a key, tap the 🔑 button, open that key's box, and tap Remove. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Information the data sources receive

To show live data, the app requests it directly from each source over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Like any website, each source receives your phone's IP address and the time of the request. The app also identifies itself as "alexc-jmapaio" to these sources, which some of them require. The app doesn't send your name, email, or device identifiers.

  • Map: OpenFreeMap supplies the base map, so it receives the map area you're viewing.
  • Keys: Each key goes only to the provider it belongs to, and only to request that provider's data.
  • Links: If you tap a link, such as a news article or a source's website, it opens in your phone's browser or email app. That site's own privacy policy applies there.

Data retention and deletion

The developer keeps no data about you, so there's nothing to delete on the developer's side. Data on your phone stays until you remove it or uninstall the app.

Children

Jmap AIO is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
